Four AI jobs, one rule set

Each AI job suggests and shows its evidence. A person decides. With every job switched off, Vision still works fully, and each card simply waits for a person.

The supervision rules

Every job follows all seven. The code holds each one.

  1. 1One off switch for each job. Empty means off, and Vision still works fully.
  2. 2It only suggests, and it always shows its evidence.
  3. 3It can only add caution. It never lowers urgency, closes or merges a card, or picks who helps.
  4. 4A person confirms.
  5. 5A failure goes to a person with a flag, after at most 3 tries.
  6. 6What it makes from personal words or audio lives in the vault until the forget date. Never in the ledger or the logs.
  7. 7Each job has a measured check on synthetic data, kept in the repository.
